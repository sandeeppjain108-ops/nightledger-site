03 — How it works

Your data stays where it lives

The standing model for the last four years: access your own QuickBooks install directly, over a VPN you control. Nothing is exported, copied, or rebuilt in a separate system.

1

You grant VPN access to your own environment

Work happens inside your existing QuickBooks Desktop, QBO or Xero install — the same one your team already uses. Access can be revoked at any time.

2

Books move overnight, your time

Reconciliations, entries and filings are worked through the India night, so they're waiting, finished, at the start of your Manitoba day.

3

You review, or your CPA reviews

For firms: working papers go back for the engagement partner's review and sign-off. Compilation and review reports are issued by your firm — that stays with your CPA.

07 — FAQ

Questions firms ask first

Where does our data actually go?

Nowhere. Work happens inside your own QuickBooks or Xero install over a VPN connection you control and can revoke at any time — nothing is exported to a separate system.

Are you a CPA?

No — we're not CPAs. This is bookkeeping and year-end file preparation — reconciliations, GST/PST filing, and Caseware working papers built to your CPA's review standard. Compilation and review reports are issued by your own CPA, not by us.

What provinces do you handle for PST?

Manitoba PST, from four years of direct filing experience. Other provincial sales taxes are taken on a case-by-case basis — ask before assuming coverage.

How does onboarding start?

A short call to scope account count, transaction volume and current backlog, followed by a fixed monthly quote and a VPN access setup on your side.
