Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

NightLedger ("we", "us", "our") provides outsourced bookkeeping, GST/PST filing, and year-end working paper preparation for Canadian CPA firms and small businesses. This policy explains how we handle information in two contexts: (1) when you visit this website, and (2) when you engage us as a client.

1. This website

This site (nightledgerbooks.com) does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts, and it does not run a contact form. The only ways to reach us from this site are:

  • Emailing us directly at sandeep.jain@nightledgerbooks.com
  • Booking a call through Calendly, a third-party scheduling service. When you book a call, Calendly collects your name, email, and scheduling details under its own privacy policy.

We do not sell, rent, or share any information collected this way with third parties for marketing purposes.

2. Client engagements

Once you engage NightLedger as a client, you may share financial records, trial balances, bank statements, or other accounting data with us so we can perform bookkeeping, reconciliation, GST/PST filing, or year-end preparation work. This information is:

  • Covered by a signed engagement letter and, where requested, a mutual non-disclosure agreement (NDA), executed before any data is shared.
  • Accessed only by team members directly assigned to your engagement.
  • Stored and processed within the accounting systems you authorize us to use (e.g. QuickBooks Online, Xero, Caseware, or your own systems), rather than on this website.
  • Never used for any purpose outside the scope of the engagement you've agreed to.

3. Cross-border data handling

NightLedger's team operates from India while serving clients primarily in Canada. Data may be accessed from India in the course of normal bookkeeping work. We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect client data during this cross-border access, and we're happy to discuss specific data-handling or residency requirements as part of your engagement agreement.

4. Data security

We limit access to client data to the individuals working on your account, use secure, authenticated access to your accounting systems, and do not retain copies of your financial data outside the systems you've authorized once an engagement ends, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

5. Your rights

You may ask us at any time what information we hold related to your engagement, request corrections, or request deletion of data we control directly (as opposed to data held in your own accounting systems). Contact us at the email below.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy or how your data is handled? Email sandeep.jain@nightledgerbooks.com.
